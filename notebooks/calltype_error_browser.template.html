How the numbers are made. Clip embeddings are mean-pooled at each encoder's reported best layer. A logistic-regression probe is trained on four fifths of the birds and tested on the rest, five times, so every prediction is for a bird the probe never saw. p is the probe's probability for the type it chose, p(true) its probability for the correct type, and rank where the correct type came among the 11.

Distances (d true, d called) are measured from the clip to the centre of the true type and of the called type. They are computed in a 10-dimensional LDA space fitted on the training birds and scaled so that 1.0 is as far out as a typical member of that type. A mistake with d called below 1 sits inside the other type's cluster.
