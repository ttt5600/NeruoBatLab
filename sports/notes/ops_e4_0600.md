## Learnings
### A live maker fill does not imply a trade-through print
KXHIGHCHI-26NOV16-T56: the live 22:00 order filled 24 @ .97 (exec journal 08:03Z), yet none of the 47 public prints
since the post was below .97 (forward log, live_q_tt_by_T false). Fills happen AT the level, so the trade-through model
(used in dev and in this paper trial) undercounts fills vs reality. It is conservative on fill rate and on adverse
selection, not neutral.
### A 06:00-D weather decision is in-play for edgelab
edgelab's PIT check needs decided_at < event_start (local midnight of D for weather). The 06:00-D add-on therefore uses
the in-play convention event_start = market close (rules/lock_in.py). Any later morning-of weather trial needs the same
convention or a vault exception.
### Executor journal fill state is up to 1 h stale
kalshi_exec reconciles hourly at :03, so "filled by T" is judged on a snapshot up to ~57 min old. The rule was frozen
that way before any data; a fill in the last hour counts as resting.
