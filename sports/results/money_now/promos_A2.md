### 3d. HOW THE BONUS PAYS OUT -- VERIFIED (official help doc) -- important for hedge math
Source: https://docs.polymarket.us/learn/faq/withdraw-promo.md (read 2026-11-20)
- "Promotional credits (shown in the app as Bonus) are for trading only and can't be withdrawn. Profits you make with them can be withdrawn."
- "How your Bonus is used: Your Cash is used first when you open a trade. If you have Bonus funds, those funds cover the losses your trades face. If you have no Cash on the account, you can use the Bonus to open trades."
- "When a trade using Bonus wins, the profit goes to your Cash balance. The Bonus you used comes back as Bonus, so you can trade with it again."
- "When any of your trades lose: Losses on any trade are taken from your Bonus first, then from your Cash."
- "Example #1: You use $20 of your Cash to buy shares at 50¢. If that position loses and you have $10 in Bonus left on the account, you will get back up to $10 added to your Cash from the Bonus funds."
- "Example #2: You use a $20 Bonus to buy shares at 50¢. If they win, the payout is $40. $20 profit goes to your Cash and can be withdrawn. $20 goes back to your Bonus."
=> STAKE NOT RETURNED as cash (bonus principal never converts), BUT the Bonus acts as a loss buffer: the first $X of losses on ANY trade (cash-funded or not) is absorbed by the Bonus, and the Bonus is recycled after a win. Consistent with filing language (Sequential terms): "the Promotional Trading Credit principal will be removed when the position is closed, settled, or liquidated; the Program Participant retains any resulting economic value in excess of the Promotional Trading Credit principal."
=> (my inference) hedge implication: a cash-funded PM position that LOSES has up to $25 of the loss refunded from Bonus; if it wins, Bonus survives for the next attempt until expiry. Near-full-face capture is plausible if a losing PM leg of >= $25 occurs before expiry. Expiry unknown (see 3c).
