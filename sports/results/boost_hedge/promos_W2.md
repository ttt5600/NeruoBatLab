### 1.2 Liquidity Incentive Program (market-maker rewards, open to all) -- LIVE (OFF + FILING PMX-2026-70)
- Sources: https://docs.polymarket.us/incentives/liquidity.md (raw_W2/pm_incentives_liquidity.md, read 11-24); overview "Liquidity Incentive Program | Open" (pm_incentives_overview.md); terms FILING https://www.cftc.gov/filings/orgrules/rules11032642175.pdf (cover) + rules11032642173.pdf (Appendix A), PMX-2026-70 dated Nov 3 2026, "The Program will be effective on November 18, 2026", "the Program will expire on November 18, 2027". - Eligibility (FILING): "Eligible Participants are Participants who are in good standing ... All Eligible Participants will be automatically enrolled for each Time Period." CA: no state exclusion stated.
- Scoring (OFF): "Every second, a random snapshot of the order book is taken. Your score for each resting order is: Score = Discount Factor ^ (ticks from best price) x Order Size". "Each side of the book is scored independently". "You do not have to quote both sides yourself; once a second qualifies, a one-sided quote still earns on the side it rests on. Programs without a Max Spread score each side on its own, even if the other side is empty." Target Size: "the minimum aggregate size of resting orders (across all participants) needed on a side of the book for that side to qualify ... All orders within that range score; orders beyond it do not." "If Target Size is reached before your price level, your order will not score".
- Payout formula (FILING): "Payout(Participant) = min( TimePeriodLiquidityProviderScore(Participant) x TimePeriodReward x QualificationFactor, RewardCap(Participant)), where a Reward Cap Percentage is specified". Optional per-market "Minimum Volume Requirement" = own Executed Notional Volume "at least 0.01% of the Executed Notional Volume of that Contract during that Time Period"; optional "Reward Cap Percentage ... no greater than 100% ... of the Participant's Executed Notional Volume". Filing bounds on pool size/target size are "[REDACTED]".
- Minimum payout / timing (OFF): "Rewards under $1.00 are not paid out." "Rewards are calculated within 5 business days of each time period ending and credited to your account within 2 business days". "No rewards are distributed for canceled or postponed games." Cash vs credit: NOT STATED in docs or filing ("credited to Eligible Participant accounts").

NO cross-venue hedging clause; hedged trades with real fills on ProphetX still change beneficial ownership.
- Stacking (FILING): other rebate programs "apply only to the fees remaining after the Fee Rebate under this Program".
