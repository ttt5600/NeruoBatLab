Encoder Roadmap

Every pretraining variant we have run, are running, or intend to run — laid out as one design space so no cell gets tested twice and none gets missed. Current state: mixing general audio back into continued pretraining just erased a catastrophic-forgetting failure, and the combined-corpus arm is the next thing to settle.

00 Where we are

The project's long-running result has been that run11 — a HuBERT pretrained on 116 h of our own colony audio — loses to AVES, the same architecture pretrained on generic animal audio. Round 1 of domain-adaptive pretraining made that worse. Round 2 fixed it.

Rehearsal closes the forgetting gap

Continuing AVES's pretraining on zebra-finch-only audio drove held-out detection below a plain energy detector. Mixing 19.3 % FSD50K — real audio from AVES's own corpus — back into the training stream, with everything else identical, reversed it.

−0.1179 ZF-only (round 1) → +0.0196 with replay (round 2)

BirdPark AP relative to run11, at the arm pre-committed on in-distribution AUC. A swing of 0.1375 — more than twice this holdout's resolution.

The honest reading. Replay is significantly better than run11 in-distribution (+0.0032 AUC, CI [+0.0012, +0.0053]). On the holdout its +0.0196 AP is not distinguishable — CI [−0.0166, +0.0709] straddles zero. So the defensible claim is that replay is the first arm that is better somewhere and worse nowhere, not that it beats run11 on transfer. The 0.1375 swing away from round 1, however, is far too large to be noise.

Selection amplifies noise 50–70×

Across a whole training trajectory, in-distribution AUC moves 0.002 while holdout AP moves 0.14. The criterion used to pick an arm carries almost no information about the axis we care about — so arm spread gets reported alongside every holdout number, and post-hoc maxima never get quoted as results.

05 Hard constraints

Things no amount of compute will fix. Worth stating so they stop being re-proposed.

AVES's corpus is ~70 % unobtainable

Of its 360 h, only FSD50K (~108 h) is distributed as audio. AudioSet and VGGSound ship YouTube IDs, and the AVES paper states outright that the corpus is not exactly reproducible. Links have rotted since 2022. An exact replication is impossible, and any scraped substitute would differ in unknowable ways.

One cross-lab holdout, four blocks wide

BirdPark is the only encoder-level holdout that is both clean and cross-lab. It can detect a failure the size of round-1 DAPT; it cannot rank checkpoints within a trajectory. The chick recordings were built out as a second axis and returned not distinguishable on every comparison — they are same-corpus, so they do not test domain shift. Real resolution needs a larger cross-lab recording set.

No bird is held out of pretraining

99.5 % of curated clips come from the 120 pretrained recordings, so leave-birds-out constrains the probe, not the encoder. Every in-distribution number carries this caveat.
